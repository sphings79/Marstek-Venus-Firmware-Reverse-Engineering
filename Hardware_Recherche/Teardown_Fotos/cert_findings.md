## Recommendations for further research

1. **Ask the manufacturer directly** – request for certification documents and technical specifications
2. **Contact local distributors** – in Germany, Benelux or the UK; they may have access to manufacturer documentation
3. **Physical analysis** – if a device is available, open it directly and photograph the chips
4. **Patent databases** – search Google Patents, USPTO for Marstek/Hamedata
5. **FCC access via proxy/VPN** – if geo-blocking is in effect
